Import surface
--------------
Every module imports the specific mathlib files it uses; no module imports all of Mathlib. The
DECLARATION COUNT below depends on this import surface, because it includes generated auxiliary
constants (_proof_n, match_n), and which of those are generated depends on the instances and simp
lemmas in scope. The count is therefore a property of this pinned configuration, not of the
mathematics.

Source check (comments stripped before scanning)
------------------------------------------------
  pattern  \bsorry\b | \badmit\b | ^\s*axiom\b | native_decide | \bunsafe\b | implemented_by
  result   none

  The scan strips /- -/ and -- comments first, so prose mentioning "sorry" or "admits" does not
  match, and a real occurrence in code cannot hide in a comment. `decide` is used in a few concrete
  finite-group computations and is kernel-checked; `native_decide` is not used anywhere.

  three was tested.

What this log does and does not establish
-----------------------------------------
It establishes that, in the pinned environment and for the sources with the blob hashes listed
above, the whole development compiles with no errors, contains no admitted proof or project-specific
axiom in code, and that every one of its declarations depends only on the three standard mathlib
axioms. It does not establish that the Lean statements say what the article says; that is the
purpose of LEAN_COVERAGE.md, which names the actual hypotheses of each result and marks what is
formalized only partly, only as a special case, or not at all.
