**How to reproduce.** From a fresh clone, first run `lake exe cache get`, which fetches the pinned

1. It records the environment.
2. It runs `lake build`.
3. It scans all Lean *code* for `sorry`, `admit`, `axiom`, `native_decide`, `unsafe` and

4. It checks that the module-wide axiom audit ran.

`#assert_standard_axioms_in_modules ObserverEquivariance` at the end of `ObserverEquivariance.lean`,

declaration of any imported `ObserverEquivariance.*` module depends on an axiom other than
`propext`, `Classical.choice` or `Quot.sound`. Auxiliary `_proof_n` and `match_n` constants are
included in the check. The companion command `#assert_standard_axioms d₁ … dₙ` checks named
declarations.

**What "verified" means.** A row is *verified* when all four of the following hold:

1. The full mathematical statement of the paper item is a Lean declaration, not only a special
   case or a docstring.
2. Its hypotheses are no stronger than the paper's.
3. It builds in the pinned environment.
4. It is covered by the module-wide standard-axiom audit.

Every declaration named below is in a module covered by that audit.

**Statuses used.**

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| verified | as defined above |
| special case only | only a concrete instance, or a specialization, of the paper's general claim is formalized |
| partial | part of the claim is formalized; the Notes say what is missing |
| not formalized | nothing in Lean; the Notes say whether it is out of scope, optional or an open gap |

**Conventions used in the tables.**

- *Context.* Unless a row says otherwise, a general declaration lives in the context
  `{S O G : Type*} [Category S] [Category O] [Group G] {p : O ⥤ S}`. The "Actual hypotheses"
  column lists only what comes on top of that context.
- *Abbreviations.* `d : OEData G p`, `m : MinSpec G p`, `σ : StrictSection p`,
  `H : Subgroup (StrictAut S)` and `θ : H →* MulAut G`.
- *Target categories.* `C` is an arbitrary category with universes independent of `O` and `S`.
- *No hidden hypotheses.* No declaration in the main development assumes that `O` or `S` is a
  groupoid, or any `IsConnected`, `Nonempty`, `Nontrivial` or commutativity hypothesis, unless
  the hypotheses column shows it. The only `Groupoid` instances are those of the concrete
  categories `Pair X` and `Lab B Q` (Core).
- *How hypotheses were checked.* The hypotheses column was checked against the source signatures,
  including the enclosing `variable`/`include` blocks. The elaborated signatures of the main
  results were also checked with `#check` during the review pass.
- *Composition order.* Lean writes `f ≫ g` and `F ⋙ G` in diagrammatic order, while the paper
  writes operator order.
  - The groups `StrictAut`, `AutBoxG`, `AutBoxGOver`, `AutBoxGθOver`, `AutEquivOver` and `HolLift`
    multiply in operator order: `(e₁ * e₂).hom = e₂.hom ⋙ e₁.hom`.
  - `Λ_comp` reads `Λ d g ⋙ Λ d h = Λ d (h * g)`, which is the paper's `Λ_h Λ_g = Λ_{hg}`.
  - In `SingleObj G`, `f ≫ g = g * f`.
  - `Equiv.Perm` multiplies as composition, `(σ * τ) x = σ (τ x)`.

## 3. Not formalized, special cases, and open points

This section is meant to be exhaustive about what the development does *not* contain. It covers
the paper material that is out of scope, the remarks with no Lean counterpart, the rows above that
are marked `partial`, and the points where a Lean statement is deliberately narrower than a
reader might assume.

### 3.1 Paper material that is out of scope

- `sec:introduction`, `sec:scope` and `sec:related`: interpretation, motivation and comparison
  with other categorical approaches. No mathematical claim of the article's own is stated there
  that is not also stated in `sec:data`–`sec:boundaries`.
- `sec:lean`, `sec:lean-r5` and `tab:lean`: the article's own description of the formalization.
  This matrix is the counterpart of that description, not a formalization target. The claims
  `sec:lean-r5` makes about the four new modules are checked against the sources in §2.8.
- The cited background result at the end of `sec:holonomy`
  (`Stab_{Gau(P)}(A) ≅ C_G(Hol_{u_0}(A))` for a smooth connection, with its two references). It
  is external literature, and INSTR states explicitly that it need not be formalized. The
  algebraic mechanism the paper says it isolates *is* formalized (`prop:holonomy`, §2.7).
- Nothing Lorentzian or Poincaré-shaped is formalized. `sec:scope` mentions a semidirect shape
  `V ⋊ O(1,3)` only as an abstract illustration; mathlib has no `O(1,3)`, and the twisted theorem
  is applied instead to the finite witnesses `ex:twist` and `zmod3SemidirectWitness`.

### 3.2 Remarks and sentences with no Lean statement

- **Diagonal orbit category** (unnumbered remark after `cor:invariant-calibration`): `O/G ≅ S × BG`
  in the chosen coordinates. No orbit category is constructed anywhere; the module docstrings of
  `Residual`, `ResidualCategory` and `Calibration` each record this omission. The two
  factorization isomorphisms that the remark summarizes are formalized (§2.4).
- **Non-abelian kernel** (remark after `thm:strict`): "the kernel itself need not be abelian; a
  general lift `Λ_b Ã` need not commute with `Λ_a`". There is no declaration asserting either
  half. The kernel is identified with `G` (`ker_ΦOver_eq_range_ΛOver`), so the first half follows
  by instantiating at a nonabelian `G`, but no lemma says so. The closest witnesses in the
  development are `ComponentsSwapExample.liftHomOver_discSwap_not_comm` (a canonical lift that
  does not commute with a translation, over a disconnected base) and the nonabelian illustration
  `s3_Λ_ne_actFunctor` / `twist_conj_r`.
- **The paragraph after `ex:nonfaithful`**: appending an extra `BK` factor to `S × Pair(G)`
  without changing the object fibers. Not formalized; only the single-factor example itself is.
- **`sec:records`, informal aside**: convention-independent statistics such as the absolute values
  of the recorded numbers remain available. Partly closed in r5. One such statistic is now
  formalized and analysed — `RecordsR4.maxProcedure` (`max_{j∈I}|y_j| · 1_I`), proved even
  (`maxProcedure_even`) and proved *not* compatible with restriction (`maxProcedure_not_natural`),
  which is the article's r5 point that availability does not imply compatibility. What is still
  not formalized is the general aside itself: there is no statement that the family of
  absolute-value (or other even) statistics is available, and no construction packaging any of
  them as a natural transformation. The impossibility half, `recInfo_no_recovery`, is formalized,
  as before.
- **`tab:assumptions`**: no declaration of its own, by nature. Its checkable content — that no
  normal-form, section, descent, residual or calibration statement carries a connectedness
  hypothesis — is visible in the hypothesis columns of §§2.2–2.4 and is recorded as a row in §2.1.

### 3.3 Rows marked `partial`, with their precise deficit

- **Coherent covariant implementations** (paragraph after `def:law`): partly closed in r5, but
  still partial. The *single-transformation* notion is now defined and classified:
  `ResidualData.InvariantImplementation D A θ` and `TwistedIntertwiner D A θ`, with the bijection
  `implementationEquiv` (`prop:data-implementation`, §2.8). What is still missing is coherence: no
  definition quantifies over a group-indexed family `{α_A}_{A ∈ H}`, and nothing relates the
  implementations of `A₁`, `A₂` and `A₁ ⋙ A₂` (§3.4). The paper's own instance in `sec:records`
  (`recα`, `recα_involution`, `recαℓ`, `recαℓ_involution`) remains the only family with a proved
  coherence law, and it is an involution rather than a general composition law. The type-level
  distinction between an action on the target and the presentation twist `θ` is unchanged.
- **Reference recovery (`sec:records`, r5 paragraph)**: `RecordsR4.reference_determines_sign` and
  `reference_recovers_value` prove the two scalar identities `(ε q_ref)/q_ref = ε` and
  `((ε q_ref)/q_ref)(ε q) = q` for `q_ref ≠ 0`. The article's sentence continues "and the original
  calibration applies": that half is not formalized. There is no statement at the level of the
  functors — no transformation `recD τ ⟹ recP ⋙ L₀ τ` built from a reference observation, and
  therefore no statement that the extra information restores exactly the recovery that
  `recInfo_no_recovery` rules out without it. Closing this would mean enlarging the raw functor to
  carry the reference value, which the development does not do.
- **`sec:holonomy`, trivial `ρ`**: `holLiftEquivTrivial : HolLift 1 ≃* G × MulAut Γ` is proved
  abstractly, but it is not connected to `productData (SingleObj Γ) G` and
  `autBoxGOverMulEquivProd`, and its compatibility with the projection to `Aut(BΓ)` is not stated.
  The paper's sentence ("trivial `ρ` recovers the untwisted product classification") is therefore
  matched in content but not by a lemma identifying the two classifications.

### 3.4 Statements that are narrower than the surrounding prose

New in r5 (the four added modules):

- **The new general results live only in the strict product normal form.** Every declaration of
  `Comparisons` and `Implementations` is about `D : ResidualData S C G` and the product model
  `S × Pair G`, via `D.presentationFunctor = residualQ S G ⋙ D.toFunctor`. `prop:comparisons`,
  `prop:data-implementation` and their corollaries are *not* restated for an arbitrary
  `d : OEData G p` and an arbitrary invariant `F : O ⥤ C`. The inherited normal form
  (`thm:normalform`, §2.2) and the residual-data correspondence (`prop:residual`, §2.4) supply the
  passage, but no lemma performs it, so a reader who wants the general-datum form has to transport
  it by hand. The article states this limitation in `sec:lean-r5`.
- **The implementation criterion covers one base transformation, not a family.**
  `implementationEquiv`, `invariant_implementation_iff` and `implementation_app` are indexed by a
  single `A : S ⥤ S` and a single `θ : MulAut G`. Nothing in the module asserts or constructs
  coherence of a family `{α_A}` under composition, and there is no statement relating
  `implementationEquiv` for `A₁ ⋙ A₂` to the ones for `A₁` and `A₂`. The article says so
  explicitly after `prop:data-implementation`; it is recorded here because the word
  "implementation" invites the stronger reading. (In the other direction the Lean statement is
  *wider* than the paper's: `A` is an arbitrary endofunctor of `S`, not required to lie in
  `H ≤ Aut(S)` or even to be invertible.)
- **The `Pair G` hom universe is pinned to `0` in the new statements.**
  `ResidualData.presentationFunctor` is defined with `residualQ.{_, _, _, 0} S G`, so every
  statement of `Comparisons` and `Implementations` is about `productData.{_, _, _, 0} S G`,
  whereas `productData` itself leaves that universe free. Nothing mathematical is lost — the
  groupoid `Pair G` has `PUnit` hom-sets in every universe — but unlike the inherited modules
  (§4, "Universes"), here the pinning *is* visible in the statements, so the new results do not
  literally apply to a `Pair G` instance taken at another hom universe without re-elaboration.
- **Orbit recovery: the negative half is not a named lemma.** `RecordsR4.orbitRecovery` is proved
  invariant and natural and to satisfy `K_I(ε q) = [q]`. The article's next sentence — that for
  nonzero `y` it identifies `y` and `-y` and is therefore not a calibration isomorphism to the
  signed data — has no declaration. It is the instance `y := -x` of `orbitClass_eq_iff`, but no
  statement of the form "`orbitRecovery` is not injective / not an isomorphism" exists.
- **Linear coinvariants are stated abstractly.** `signRelations`, `signRelations_eq_top` and
  `linear_coinvariants_subsingleton` quantify over an arbitrary real vector space. They are never
  instantiated at `recordSpace τ I`, so the sentence "for these record spaces the coinvariant
  quotient is zero" is a one-line instantiation that the sources do not carry out.
- **`ex:complex-obstruction` is pinned to universe 0.** `complexData` and `realData` are stated for
  `ModuleCat.{0} ℂ` and `ModuleCat.{0} ℝ`, and the obstruction is proved at the base object
  `⟨0⟩ : Pair (Fin 2)`. Neither restricts the content (a non-existence statement needs only one
  object, and the target category is fixed by the example), but the statements are not universe
  polymorphic.

Inherited:

- **`prop:cartesian`, "split Grothendieck fibration"**: `OEData.chi_split_cartesian` proves the
  content (each `χ` is `IsCartesianOver`, plus the four splitting laws) as an explicit
  conjunction. No mathlib fibration class is instantiated and none is claimed; the docstring says
  so.
- **`rigidity` is stated over `A : S ≌ S`**, not over `StrictAut S`, and the library contains no
  `StrictAut S → S ≌ S` bridge. The group-level consequence that the paper actually uses is
  stated separately (`existsUnique_ΛHom_mul_of_Φ_eq`, `existsUnique_ΛHomOver_mul_of_ΦOver_eq`,
  `existsUnique_ΛHomθOver_mul_of_ΦθOver_eq`).
- **Base change for the full-group twisted type.** `autBoxGθOverCongr` and all the
  `*_eq_of_base` / `*_eq_conj_of_base` lemmas exist for `AutBoxGOver` and `AutBoxGθOver d H θ`.
  The legacy full-group type `AutBoxGθ d θ` (with `θ : StrictAut S →* MulAut G`) has no
  congruence isomorphism and no base-change lemmas.
- **Components versus `thm:strict`.** `autBoxGOverMulEquivProdOfComponents` (connected base, via
  `prop:components`) is not proved equal to the independently constructed
  `autBoxGOverMulEquivProd`. The paper claims no such comparison.
- **Holonomy, `Q₈`.** `holQ8_no_section` and `holQ8_orderOf_lift` are stated for `E_ρ → H_ρ`.
  There is no transported corollary for `HolLift ρ → StrictAut (SingleObj Q₈)`, and no lemma
  stating the commuting square `holQ8HρEquiv ∘ EρToHρ = holQ8Quot ∘ holQ8EρEquiv` between the two
  identifications. Morphism-level equivariance of a holonomy lift (an `IsHolLift.map_act`
  companion to `IsHolLift.map_holChi`) is likewise not stated.
- **`def:vertical-trivial`, redundancy of clause (i)**: proved from `StrictSection` and from
  `MinSpec`; there is no `OEData`-named wrapper (use `d.strictSection`).
- **Section formulation of `lem:local`**: `preservesCleavage_iff_exists_section` gives existence
  of the constant `c`, matching the paper's "there is one `c`"; uniqueness of `c` is not stated
  separately (it follows from `ΛHom_injective` when `S` is nonempty).
- **`ex:nonfaithful`**: the lift group is a subgroup of `StrictAut (SingleObj K)` over the
  identity base rather than a group of pairs `(F, A)` with a projection `Φ`; the base component is
  forced to be trivial (`strictAut_discretePUnit_eq_one`), so the two readings agree here. The
  ambient types are pinned to `PUnit.{1}`; generalizing the universe was not done and is cosmetic.

## 4. Representation choices

These are places where a faithful formalization still had to choose an encoding. Each choice is
recorded here so that a reader can tell what a Lean statement does and does not say.

**Labeled versus unlabeled law data (`sec:records`).** The paper's sentence "for these explicitly
labeled vector spaces, `LA ≠ L` strictly" is formalized twice. The labeled reading is
`Lℓ τ : RecDom ⥤ Pair RecDom × ModuleCat ℝ`, `Lℓ = recLabel.prod' (L₀ τ)`, with
`Lℓ_comp_snd : Lℓ τ ⋙ Prod.snd = L₀ τ` recovering the unlabeled functor; there
`recA_comp_Lℓ_ne` holds for every `τ`. But the labels, together with the thinness of the base,
force `strictLawStabilizer (Lℓ τ) = ⊥` (`strictLawStabilizer_Lℓ_eq_bot`, stated for `Lℓ τ` only),
so the labeled inequality would hold for *any* labeled functor of this form and says nothing about
the free-particle law. The unlabeled statement `recA_comp_L₀_ne` (for
`τ ≠ 0`) is therefore also proved, by evaluating the concrete record `x_j = j` (`recIdxRecord`)
after the reversal; it uses linearity of the `ModuleCat` casts (`L₀_eqToHom_hom_apply_of_eq`).
INSTR §6E asks precisely that the difference be documented rather than papered over with a failed
`rfl`.

**Signs as `ℤˣ`.** The convention group is `G = ℤˣ`, not an abstract two-element group: signs act
on record spaces by the real scalar `((ε : ℤ) : ℝ) •`, which makes the residual action literally
the sign representation. `recSignMulEquiv : ℤˣ ≃* Multiplicative (ZMod 2)` connects it to the
`C₂` of the paper's statements (`recSym`).

**The time step `τ`.** `L₀ τ`, `recD τ`, `recC τ` and the information-condition lemmas are stated
for an arbitrary `τ : ℝ`, which is more general than the paper's `τ > 0`. Positivity is assumed
only where it is used: `mem_recordSpace_full_iff` (`0 < τ`) for the description
`q₂ - 2q₁ + q₀ = 0` of `L(T)`, and `τ ≠ 0` for `recIdxRecord` and `recA_comp_L₀_ne`. At `τ = 0`
the functor `L₀ 0` is not the paper's `L` (records are constants), and nothing downstream claims
otherwise.

**`Pair PUnit` as the terminal base.** `ex:invariant` takes `S = *`. In Lean this is `Pair PUnit`
(one object, one arrow) rather than `Discrete PUnit`; `pairPUnit_obj_eq` and
`pairPUnit_functor_eq` prove the terminality that the example uses. The same base carries the
`S₃` left-versus-right illustration of `ex:twist`.

**`Fin 2` as the two-object base.** The example after `prop:nocleavage` uses `Fin 2` with
mathlib's `Preorder.smallCategory` instance, so the single nonidentity arrow `0 ⟶ 1` is
`homOfLE`; `hom_one_zero_isEmpty` and `arrow01_not_isIso` record that the base has a genuinely
noninvertible arrow and no arrow back. `strictAut_eq_one` and `top_eq_bot` record that its strict
automorphism group is trivial, which is why the example is stated with `H = ⊤`.

**`NormalizedPreDatum` does not include invertibility of `ℓ`.** The structure carries (N1)–(N5)
except the invertibility part of (N5); `OEData` extends it with the single field `p_faithful`
(N6), and `OEData.ltrans_isIso` then *derives* invertibility from `p_ltrans` plus full
faithfulness. This is what makes `ex:nonfaithful` expressible: `nonfaithfulPreDatum` is a
`NormalizedPreDatum` with an explicit `IsIso` instance for `ℓ`, so (N1)–(N5) hold there in full
while (N6) fails. Since `OEData extends NormalizedPreDatum`, the projections are
`NormalizedPreDatum.act`, `NormalizedPreDatum.base`, and so on; dot notation `d.act`, `d.base` is
unchanged.

**The redundant field `act_free`.** `NormalizedPreDatum` has a freeness field that is not a clause
of `def:data`; it is derivable from the other fields (`lem:coordinates`, proved in
`OEData.coordinate_identities`). The source marks it REDUNDANT and says so in its comment. It
therefore adds no hypothesis, but it is a field, so a reader checking "are the Lean hypotheses the
paper's?" should know that this one is provable from the rest rather than assumed.

**Weak versus strict normal form.** `thm:normalform` is formalized as a pair of strict functor
equalities (`normalFormTo_comp_normalFormFrom`, `normalFormFrom_comp_normalFormTo`); the packaged
equivalences `productNormalForm : O ≌ S × Pair G` and `projectionEquivalence : O ≌ S` are kept
from the earlier development as the weak form. The strict form is the one the paper's argument
needs, because strict automorphism groups are not transported along a mere equivalence; the
equivalences are stated for convenience and for `cor:section`'s "hence `p` is an equivalence".

**Groups as subgroups or as structures.** `AutBoxG d` is a structure with a `Group` instance and
`AutBoxGOver d H := Subgroup.comap (Φ d) H` is literally `Φ⁻¹(H)`; `AutEquivOver d H` (transport
dropped) and `HolLift ρ` are a structure and a subgroup of a product of `StrictAut` groups
respectively. In `ex:nonfaithful` the lift group is a subgroup of `StrictAut (SingleObj K)` rather
than a group of pairs `(F, A)`; `strictAut_discretePUnit_eq_one` shows the base component would be
forced to be the identity in any case.

**Nonabelian `H¹`.** `OECocycle.H1` is a local quotient of the set of cocycles by the
cohomologous relation, with `H1.trivialClass` and `H1.toPointed` as the pointed structure.
mathlib's group cohomology does not cover the nonabelian coefficients the paper needs, and INSTR
permits a local definition. The extension `G ⋊[θ] H` is fixed throughout, as in the paper.

**Universes.** `Pair X` gets its `Groupoid` instance with `Hom _ _ := PUnit`, whose universe is
left free. That free hom universe propagates: for example `HolTotal Γ G` carries an extra free
universe parameter, which is why some proofs in `Examples/Holonomy` inline an argument instead of
citing a lemma, and why `swapAut_obj` in `Examples/Twist` is proved as `⟨rfl, rfl⟩`. No *statement*
was weakened to accommodate this; the workarounds are inside proofs. In `Examples/Records` the
functors are pinned to `Functor.{0,0}` and `ModuleCat.{0} ℝ`, which is forced by `RecDom` being a
`SmallCategory`; `recD_not_factors` quantifies over that universe only, and nothing is lost,
because the target category is fixed by the example. `ResidualCategory` pins the hom universe of
`Pair G` to `0` inside its constructions, and there the pinning does not appear in the statements.
In the r5 modules `Comparisons` and `Implementations` it does appear in the statements, because
`ResidualData.presentationFunctor` is defined with `residualQ.{_, _, _, 0}`; see §3.4.

**Composition and multiplication order.** Recorded in §1: Lean writes `f ≫ g` and `F ⋙ G`
diagrammatically, while the groups here multiply in operator order. Every row above that mentions
a conjugation or a semidirect law was checked against that convention.
